    Quote

    You send the loading port, the discharge port and the cargo. The agent checks the sailings and replies with options and prices.

  2. 2

    Book

    It picks the carrier and the sailing, reserves the space and confirms the cut-off dates with you.

  3. 3

    Clear

    It prepares the customs entries, matches the paperwork to the cargo and files them.

  4. 4

    Track

    It follows the vessel from port to port and warns you about a delay the moment it appears.

  5. 5

    Release

    The cargo is released at the discharge port and every document ends up in one place, ready for your accounts.

Three ways to fill a container. One agent for all of them.

Full container

A whole box for your cargo. The agent books the container and the sailing, then follows the vessel and plans around the port.

Books
Carrier, container size and sailing date.
Files
Bill of lading, customs entry and port documents.
Watches
Vessel position, port congestion and cut-off times.

Part load

Less than a container. The agent finds a consolidator, books your space and follows the box you share.

Books
Consolidator, cargo volume and sailing date.
Files
House bill of lading, packing list and customs entry.
Watches
Warehouse cut-off, vessel position and unloading at arrival.

Refrigerated

Cargo that has to stay cold. The agent books a reefer unit and keeps an eye on the set temperature all the way.

Books
Reefer unit, power supply and set temperature.
Files
Bill of lading, health certificate and customs entry.
Watches
Temperature log, vessel position and power at the port.
