Questions

Before you ship.

What does the agent do, and what do I decide?

It reads the paperwork, checks the sailings, prepares the customs entries and follows the vessel. When a choice changes what you pay or when the cargo arrives, it stops and asks you.

What do you ship?

Sea freight only. Full containers, part loads and refrigerated containers. We do not handle air or road freight.

Is customs included?

Yes. The agent prepares the customs entries, matches the paperwork to the cargo and files them.

What do I need to get a quote?

The loading port, the discharge port and what the cargo is. The agent asks for anything else it needs.

What does .si stand for?

Superintelligence. It is not a country code here.

Tell us where it needs to be.

Fill in what you know. The agent needs two ports and a cargo to start, and it asks for the rest.
